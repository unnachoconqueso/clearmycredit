Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 26, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. ClearMyCredit uses only essential browser storage mechanisms (localStorage) and does not set third‑party tracking cookies.

2. How We Use Storage

We use localStorage (a modern browser feature) to temporarily store the information you enter in our form. This data never leaves your device and is automatically deleted after 24 hours of inactivity. We do not use persistent cookies for analytics, advertising, or any tracking purpose.

3. Your Choices

You can clear localStorage at any time by clearing your browser’s site data or using your browser’s developer tools. Disabling localStorage may prevent the form from saving your progress.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@clearmycredit.com.
