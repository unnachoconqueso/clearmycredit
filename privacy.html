Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 25, 2026

ClearMyCredit ("we," "our," or "us") provides a self‑help tool to generate credit dispute letters. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information on our servers. All data you enter (name, address, creditor details, etc.) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

We use Stripe for payment processing. Stripe may collect billing information as described in Stripe's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use it for any purpose. The tool uses your inputs solely to generate a letter on your device.

Third‑Party Services

Our website links to Stripe for payment. Stripe may use cookies or collect data as described in their privacy policy. We do not control Stripe’s data practices.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the tool from unauthorized access, but because all data remains on your device, you are responsible for the security of your own browser and device.

Children’s Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the tool constitutes acceptance of any changes.
