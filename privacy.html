Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 26, 2026

ClearMyCredit ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, address, city, state, ZIP code that you enter in our dispute letter form.
  • Dispute Information: Creditor names, account numbers, dispute reasons, and explanations you provide.
  • Payment Information: We do not collect or store your payment information. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.
  • Technical Information: Browser type, IP address, and device information (collected automatically).

2. How We Store Your Data

Important: Your personal and dispute information is stored locally in your browser using localStorage technology. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • We do not have access to your information
  • Your data is automatically deleted after 24 hours of inactivity
  • You can clear your data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Generate your credit dispute letter
  • Process your payment through Stripe
  • Improve our website and services

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information with:

  • Stripe: For payment processing only
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers
  • Regular security audits

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (stored locally in your browser)
  • Delete your data (clear browser localStorage)
  • Opt-out of any future communications

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for session management. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

We use Stripe for payment processing. Please review Stripe's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle your data.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@clearmycredit.com

By using ClearMyCredit, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
