🔒 Trust Center

Your privacy and security are our priority. Here’s how we protect you.

Data Privacy

All information you enter in our form is stored exclusively on your own device using browser localStorage. We never receive, store, or process your personal data on our servers. Your data is automatically deleted after 24 hours of inactivity.

Secure Payments

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe, a PCI‑compliant payment processor. We never see or store your credit card information. Stripe handles all sensitive data with bank‑level security.

Encrypted Connection

Every page on ClearMyCredit is served over HTTPS (SSL/TLS), ensuring that all communication between your browser and our site is encrypted and cannot be intercepted.

No Third‑Party Tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising, or any third‑party scripts that track your behavior across websites. Your visit is private.

Compliance

Our dispute letter templates are designed to align with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). However, we are not a law firm and provide self‑help tools only. For legal advice, please consult a qualified attorney.

Questions?

If you have any security concerns, reach us at security@clearmycredit.com.
